Privacy Policy — Buskit Tools

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Buskit (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Buskit Tools Android app (also known as Buskit Live FX, “the App”) and this website. Buskit Tools is a real-time audio effects and music tools app for musicians. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information that stays on your device

The following is processed or stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Audio. The App uses the microphone (or an audio input you connect) to process sound in real time and to run features such as the tuner. Audio is processed on your device only. We do not upload, store or share your audio.
  • Recordings. If you use the recording feature, the files are saved on your device (Music/Buskit/Records). They are never sent to us.
  • License token. After activation, a digitally signed license token is kept on your device in encrypted storage. It is removed when you log out.

2. Information sent to our server

To activate and verify your license, the App communicates with our licensing server over HTTPS and sends:

  • A device identifier: an irreversible hash (SHA-256) calculated from your Android ID and basic device model details (board, brand, hardware, manufacturer). We do not receive your raw Android ID.
  • A hardware signature: a hash of the device model details only.
  • The App’s package name, its version/update number and generation.
  • The license code you enter (including the free-trial code) or, for a purchase, the Google Play purchase token and product ID.

The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or files.

3. How we use information

  • To activate, verify and restore licenses, and to keep a license tied to the device it was activated on.
  • To enforce the one-time free trial and prevent abuse.
  • To confirm purchases made through Google Play.
  • To tell you when an app update is available, and to fix problems.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Payments

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. Google provides us with a purchase token and order confirmation, which our server verifies with the Google Play Developer API.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or rent your information. Data is processed only by providers needed to run the service: Google (Google Play Billing and Developer API, and Firebase / Cloud Firestore, where license and device records are stored) and our server hosting provider (Render). We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Retention

License, device and purchase records are kept while your license is active and as long as needed for support, accounting and abuse prevention. The record showing that a device has used the free trial is kept after you log out, so the trial cannot be repeated. You can request deletion at any time (see section 11).

7. Security

Communication with our server uses HTTPS, license tokens are digitally signed, and the token stored on your device is encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Permissions used by the App

  • Microphone — real-time audio processing and the tuner.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — to show the ongoing notification while the audio engine runs. Optional.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) — to save your recordings to the Music folder.
  • Internet — license activation and verification.

9. This website

This website may use Firebase (Google) to count visits and downloads anonymously and to store reviews and questions you choose to post publicly. If you submit a purchase request or contact us, the details you provide (such as name, email, phone number and message) are used only to respond to you and process your order. The site loads resources such as fonts, scripts and flag images from third-party CDNs, which may receive your IP address.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or Turkey’s KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, email us at the address below. Note that deleting a license record may deactivate that license.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact
